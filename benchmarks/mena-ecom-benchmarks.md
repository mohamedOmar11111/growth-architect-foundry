## Delivery & Confirmation Rates (COD)

| Market | Confirmation rate | Delivery rate | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Egypt | 70-80% | 60-75% | Industry heuristic — aggregated from 3 MENA e-com operators (2025) |
| KSA | 80-90% | 75-85% | Industry heuristic — same source |
| UAE | 85-95% | 80-90% | Industry heuristic — same source |
| Kuwait | 80-90% | 75-85% | Industry heuristic — same source |

**Note:** These are NOT benchmarks to judge your account against. Every account has its own baseline. Use these only to sanity-check whether your rates are in the right ballpark. If your KSA delivery rate is 60%, something is wrong — but "wrong" might be logistics, not marketing.

**Source methodology:** Aggregated from anonymized data shared by 3 MENA direct-to-consumer operators across beauty, fashion, and home categories. Not a representative sample — selection bias toward operators who share data.
