## WhatsApp-Specific CTAs

| EN | AR | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| Message us on WhatsApp for instant reply | راسلنا على واتساب نرد عليك فوراً | Click-to-WhatsApp ads |
| Send "OFFER" to get the deal | ابعت "عرض" على واتساب تحصل على العرض | WhatsApp automation |
| Confirm your order on WhatsApp | أكّد طلبك على واتساب | COD confirmation flow |
| Track your order on WhatsApp | تابع طلبك على واتساب | Post-purchase |

---

*CTAs are not interchangeable. A conversion CTA on an awareness campaign wastes money. Match the CTA to the funnel stage.*
