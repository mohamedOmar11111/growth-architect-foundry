## Brand Overview
Aurora Skincare — direct-to-consumer clinical skincare, mid-market price
point ($30-60/unit), sold through their own Shopify store and Google Ads.
Core product line: vitamin-C serum, retinol night cream, SPF daily
moisturizer. Primary acquisition channel: Google Search + Shopping.

## Target Audience
Women 28-45, skincare-conscious, price-sensitive but ingredient-literate —
they read INCI lists before buying. Responds to clinical/dermatologist
language over lifestyle/aspirational language.

## Voice
Clinical-confident, not luxury-aspirational. Lead with the active
ingredient and the mechanism, not the feeling. Avoid "glow," "radiant,"
"transform your skin" — every claim needs a concentration percentage or a
named ingredient behind it. Short sentences. No exclamation points.

## Active Directives

### Google Ads
- PREFER: exact-match on ingredient-specific queries ("vitamin c serum 20%",
  "retinol 0.5 cream") over broad category terms — this audience searches by
  active ingredient, not by benefit.
- AVOID: bidding on "anti-aging" as a standalone term — too broad, too much
  luxury-brand competition, poor CTR historically on this account type.

### Content
- PREFER: comparison/ingredient-education angles ("vitamin C vs niacinamide
  for X") over generic "5 tips for glowing skin" listicles.
- CONSTRAINT: every content brief must cite a specific active ingredient or
  concentration — no generic skincare advice with no product tie-in.

### Budget Allocation
- Google Ads: 70% of paid spend (primary channel, proven).
- Meta: 30% (retargeting only, not cold acquisition — cold Meta traffic has
  historically converted poorly for this account).

## Decision Log
- 2026-08-15 — Confirmed "anti-aging" exclusion after 3 months of data
  showing 0.8% CTR / no conversions on that term vs 4.2% CTR on
  ingredient-specific terms in the same campaign.
