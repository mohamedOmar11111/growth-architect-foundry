# Framework: Bilingual Content System

> How to write content that works in both English and Arabic without sounding like a translation. The rule: Arabic is the native language, English is the code-switching layer for platform terms.

---

## The Principle

In MENA marketing, the audience thinks in Arabic but searches in a mix. A post that's 100% Arabic reads like a government memo. A post that's 100% English reads like a foreign brand trying too hard. The sweet spot: **Arabic prose with English platform/industry terms naturally code-switched in.**

## The Three Registers

### 1. MSA (فصحى) — Formal Arabic
**Use for:** reports, whitepapers, official communications, LinkedIn long-form
**Avoid for:** social media captions, WhatsApp, TikTok — it reads like a press release
**Example:** «حققنا عائد على الإنفاق الإعلاني يبلغ 6.2× هذا الشهر»

### 2. عامية (Ammiya) — Colloquial Arabic
**Use for:** social captions, WhatsApp scripts, TikTok/Reels, community engagement
**Market-specific:** Egyptian عامية ≠ Gulf عامية ≠ Levantine عامية
**Example (Egyptian):** «الإعلانات بتاعتنا جابتلك 6x رجع على الفلوس اللي دفعناها 🔥»
**Example (Gulf):** «إعلاناتنا جابت لك 6x رجع على المبلغ اللي دفعناه 🔥»

### 3. Bilingual (Code-switched)
**Use for:** ad copy, reports to bilingual clients, internal team communication
**Pattern:** Arabic prose + English terms in parentheses or naturally placed
**Example:** «الـ ROAS حققناه 6.2× — الأعلى في آخر 3 شهور. الـ CPA انخفض 18% عن الشهر اللي فات.»

## English Terms to NEVER Translate

These terms are used in English even in Arabic-only content, because the audience searches for them in English:

| Term | Why not translate |
|---|---|
| ROAS, CPA, CTR, CPC, CPM | Industry standard — Arabic translation confuses |
| Pixel, CAPI | Technical — no common Arabic equivalent |
| CBO, Advantage+, PMax | Platform-specific product names |
| Landing page | Understood universally |
| Retargeting | Used in English across MENA |
| UGC | Used in English |
| WhatsApp Business | Brand name |

## Terms That NEED Translation

| English | Arabic | Context |
|---|---|---|
| Break-even | نقطة التعادل | Reports, economics |
| Budget | ميزانية | Client-facing |
| Target audience | الجمهور المستهدف | Strategy docs |
| Conversion rate | معدل التحويل | Reports |
| Ad spend | الإنفاق الإعلاني | Reports |
| Return on investment | العائد على الاستثمار | Formal reports |

## Dialect Guide (Quick Reference)

| Expression | MSA | Egyptian | Gulf |
|---|---|---|---|
| "This ad performed well" | حقق هذا الإعلان أداءً جيداً | الإعلان ده كان كويس | الإعلان هذا كان زين |
| "Cut the budget" | قلل الميزانية | قلل الميزانية | قلل الميزانية |
| "The CPA is too high" | الـ CPA مرتفع جداً | الـ CPA عالي أوي | الـ CPA وايد عالي |
| "Check the report" | راجع التقرير | شوف التقرير | شف التقرير |

## The Rule

**Write the way a MENA media buyer talks.** They say "الـ CPA" not "تكلفة الاستحواذ على العميل". They say "الـ ROAS" not "العائد على الإنفاق الإعلاني". If you wouldn't say it in a client call, don't write it in the report.
