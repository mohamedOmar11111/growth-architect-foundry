# Methodology: Automation & Agentic Orchestration

## Sensory Automation (The Eyes)
- **Tools:** Scrapling, WebSearch, and **gogcli**.
- **Google Integration:** Use `gogcli sheets` to monitor live market data and `gogcli gmail` to track industry news alerts.
- **Workflow:** Continuous monitoring of MENA real estate portals and C-Suite changes.
- **Trigger:** Automatic update of `intel/` files and shared Google Sheets when a significant market shift occurs.

## Operational Automation (The Muscle)
- **Tool:** Superpowers (Subagent-Driven Development).
- **Communication:** Use `gogcli chat` or `gogcli gmail` for autonomous notifications to the Architect or team members.
- **Workflow:** 
    1. **Brainstorm:** Explore growth angles.
    2. **Design:** Map the technical/strategic architecture.
    3. **Plan:** Detailed execution roadmap.
    4. **Implement:** Write code/content.
    5. **Review:** Sanity check against `architect_identity.md`.

## Cognitive Automation (The Brain)
- **Tool:** Context Engineering and **Prompt Engineering Protocol**.
- **Reference:** Utilizing `system_prompts_leaks` industry benchmarks for agent behavior refinement.
- **Workflow:** Managing long-term memory via the `memory/` log and `projects/` history.
- **Maintenance:** Compressing old conversation logs into "Actionable Lessons" every 10 sessions.
- **Intelligence Refinement:** Regularly updating agent instructions based on top-tier AI configuration patterns (Claude, Gemini, GPT-4).

## Deployment Automation
- **Interface:** Automatic generation of "Vibrant" HTML reports.
- **Archiving:** Automatically sync project deliverables to Google Drive via `gogcli drive`.
- **Scheduling:** Sync content calendars and growth sprints to Google Calendar via `gogcli calendar`.
- **Outreach:** Execute email campaigns directly through Gmail with open-tracking enabled.
