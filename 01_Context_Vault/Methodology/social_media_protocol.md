# Methodology: Social Media & Content Engineering

## Platform-Specific Protocols

### 1. TikTok & Reels (The Visual Hook)
- **Strategy:** High energy, immediate problem identification.
- **Content:** Shorter, punchier copy (Egyptian Ammiya preferred for local projects).
- **Visual Direction:** 70% of effort. Focus on POV shots, AR overlays, and rapid transitions.

### 2. Instagram (The Aesthetic Proof)
- **Strategy:** Social proof and "Vibe" management.
- **Content:** Lifestyle-oriented, aspirational language.
- **Visual Direction:** High-quality photography, clean graphic design with "Vibrant" colors.

### 3. LinkedIn (The Authority Engine)
- **Strategy:** Data-driven insights and B2B partnership building.
- **Content:** Professional English/Arabic mix. Focus on ROI, MLS integrity, and market trends.
- **Visual Direction:** Infographics, market dashboards, and executive portraits.

### 4. Facebook (The Community Hub)
- **Strategy:** Building trust and managing direct leads.
- **Content:** Conversational, long-form stories that solve specific "Pain Points."
- **Visual Direction:** Interactive carousels and "Before vs. After" growth charts.

## Content Production Workflow
1. **Identify Pain Point** (from `intel/`)
2. **Select Platform Protocol**
3. **Draft Written Copy** (Vibrant & Friendly)
4. **Draft Visual Direction** (Detailed cues for designers)
