# Automation & Tooling

The HQ's technical infrastructure is divided into sensory, operational, and cognitive automation layers.

## Technical Layers
- **Sensory (The Eyes):** Scrapling, Brave Search API, and `gogcli` for monitoring market shifts and industry news.
- **Operational (The Muscle):** Superpowers framework for subagent-driven development (Brainstorm ➔ Design ➔ Plan ➔ Implement ➔ Review).
- **Cognitive (The Brain):** Context Engineering and Prompt Engineering Protocols based on industry benchmarks.

## Deployment Automation
- **HTML Reports:** Automatic generation of vibrant, high-status reports for stakeholders.
- **Syncing:** Automated synchronization of deliverables to Google Drive and growth sprints to Google Calendar.
