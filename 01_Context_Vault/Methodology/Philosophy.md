# System Philosophy: Onyx Noir

**Onyx Noir** is the visual and strategic soul of the Growth Architect HQ. It is not just an aesthetic; it is an engine of strategic inevitability.

## Core Tenets
- **Weighted Negative Space:** Utilizing monumental void to force attention toward the "Prime Artifact."
- **Architectural precision:** Forms are sharp, crystalline, and unapologetically geometric.
- **Spectrum of Authority:** Palette of Midnight Black, Matte Charcoal, and Titanium Silver with rare "Electric Amber" strikes.
- **Anamorphic Scaling:** Tension between tiny clinical labels and massive typography.

## Strategic Impact
The philosophy reinforces the identity of the **Growth Architect**: one who sees the atoms and the stars simultaneously. Every pixel is a decision, leading the viewer through a sequence of discovery rather than a barrage of information.
