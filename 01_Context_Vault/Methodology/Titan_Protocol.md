# Titan Core Protocol v2.0

The **Titan Core Protocol** governs all operations within the HQ, ensuring a standardized, high-status execution loop.

## The SEED ➔ PAUL Loop

### STAGE 0: [SEED] — Incubation & Vision Lock
- **Comprehensive Harvest:** Exhaustive read of client context.
- **Calculate Status:** 60/40 Status Score (Authority vs. Direct Response).
- **Lock Theme:** Selection of one of the 5 Architectural Themes (e.g., Onyx Midnight).

### STAGE 1: [PAUL] — PLAN (The Architecture)
- **Command Cascade:** Drafting the Master Brief.
- **Agent Roster:** Selection of strike teams from the 36 specialized agents.

### STAGE 2: [PAUL] — APPLY (The Content Factory)
- **Ignite Assembly Line:** Transition to physical asset generation.
- **Validation Gate:** Reviewing agent outputs against the Brand Token Vault.

### STAGE 3: [PAUL] — UNIFY (The Synthesis)
- **Asset Fusion:** Compiling all strategic data and reports.
- **The Final Artifact:** Deployment of the DCL v3.0 Interactive Dashboard.
