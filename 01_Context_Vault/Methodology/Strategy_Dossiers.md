# Master Strategy Dossiers

Strategic intelligence dossiers define the core thesis and tactical
roadmaps for a specific tenant. This file holds the generic dossier
FORMAT and a fully anonymized illustrative example — a real tenant's
actual dossier belongs in that tenant's own `STRATEGY.md` under
`02_Active_Clients/<tenant>/`, never here.

## Dossier Format

A useful strategy dossier answers, per tenant:
- **Core Thesis:** the one-sentence positioning bet — what you're claiming
  against the market, not a feature list.
- **Psychological Profiles (ICPs):** what actually drives each buyer
  persona's decision (fear, ambition, loss-aversion, status) — not just
  their job title.
- **Competitive Displacement Strategy:** which specific incumbent weakness
  you're positioning against, and why that weakness is real and durable,
  not assumed.

## Illustrative Example (Anonymized — Not a Real Tenant)

**Fictional B2B tech-services company, "Positioning as the Agile
Integrator":**
- **Core Thesis:** startup speed combined with enterprise-grade security —
  positioned against slower, hourly-billing incumbents.
- **ICP A — "The Panicked Decision-Maker":** driven by fear of compliance
  failure or falling behind competitors technologically.
- **ICP B — "The Reluctant Modernizer":** driven by loss-aversion — losing
  market share to more digitally mature competitors.
- **Displacement Angle:** target incumbents whose actual weakness is slow,
  hourly-billed delivery — not a generic "we're better" claim, a specific,
  provable structural weakness in how competitors operate.
