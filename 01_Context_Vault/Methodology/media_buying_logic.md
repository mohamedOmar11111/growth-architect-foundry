# Methodology: Media Buying & Performance Logic

## The Spending Engine ($100 Daily Base)
- **Broker Acquisition ($30):** Awareness & Platform Education. Target CPA: $2 per signup.
- **Investor Lead Gen ($60):** High-Intent Conversion. Target CPA: $10-15 per qualified lead.
- **Retargeting ($10):** Pixel-based follow-ups to close the decision gap.

## Bidding & Optimization
- **Primary Strategy:** Lowest Cost (Auto-bidding) to maximize volume within budget.
- **Optimization Windows:** 7-Day Click / 1-Day View.
- **Metric Priority:** 
    1. Lead Quality (High Intent)
    2. CTR (Click-Through Rate) > 1.5%
    3. ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) for investors.

## Audience Intelligence
- Always cross-reference `intel/decision_makers.md` for B2B lookalike modeling.
- Focus on high-income segments for Investors (Top 5% Household Income).
- Exclude "Already Converted" users to prevent budget waste.
