# Precision Protocol

The **Precision Protocol** is the core growth methodology used by Mohamed Omar to build closed-loop revenue engines.

## The Three Phases
1. **Deconstruct:** Breaking down business models to their atomic unit economics. CAC/LTV is the only metric that matters.
2. **Engineer:** Building custom automation, agentic workflows, and high-conversion UX infrastructure.
3. **Scale:** Deploying media science and algorithmic ad buying to dominate the market.

## Key Principles
- **Unit Economics First:** Impressions are vanity; profit is sanity.
- **Agent-Native:** Humans design the systems; AI executes the micro-tasks.
- **Algorithmic Dominance:** Winning via mathematical rigor and signal-based testing.
